Simulated Paper-Trading Risk

Paper trading is hypothetical and does not involve real money or actual trade execution.

Simulations may not accurately reflect:

  • Liquidity and available order size.
  • Bid-and-ask spreads and slippage.
  • Partial fills, rejected orders, or execution delays.
  • Commissions, taxes, fees, and subscription costs.
  • Market halts, closures, and volatility interruptions.
  • Margin rules and brokerage restrictions.
  • Emotional and behavioral effects of real losses.

Past performance, historical relationships, paper-trading results, and backtests do not predict or guarantee future results.
