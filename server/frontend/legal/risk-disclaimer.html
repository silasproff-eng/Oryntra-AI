Market Analysis Risk Disclaimer

1. No investment advice

Oryntra does not provide personalized investment, financial, legal, accounting, or tax advice. Terms such as bullish, bearish, buy, sell, long, short, confidence, entry, stop, target, or risk/reward describe software classifications and educational scenarios only.

2. Indicator, pattern, and AI risk

Technical indicators are backward-looking transformations of market data. Pattern detection involves thresholds and approximations. Multiple indicators can derive from the same underlying prices and therefore are not independent confirmation. AI explanations may summarize incorrectly, omit context, hallucinate, or overstate confidence. A high historical score does not establish a durable edge.

3. Trading and market risk

Securities can lose value rapidly. Gaps, volatility, liquidity, halts, corporate actions, news, spreads, slippage, fees, taxes, leverage, short-sale constraints, and changing market regimes can invalidate any plan. A stop level does not guarantee an exit price, and a target does not guarantee a fill or profit.

4. Simulated trade journal

Oryntra's paper-trade journal is a user-entered educational record. It is not a brokerage order, exchange simulation, or proof of executable performance. Simulations may omit latency, bid/ask spreads, partial fills, borrow costs, commissions, market impact, and behavioral factors.

5. Market-data and chart risk

Provider data can be delayed, corrected, adjusted, incomplete, or unavailable. Oryntra may use a different source, timing convention, corporate-action treatment, or session definition than the independently embedded TradingView chart. Differences do not necessarily indicate an error. Never infer that Oryntra is providing a real-time quote or official execution price.

6. Technology risk

Internet, device, backend, database, provider, AI, notification, and chart failures may interrupt or alter results. Cached analysis can become stale. Alerts may arrive late or not at all.

7. Your responsibility

You are solely responsible for independently verifying information and deciding whether, when, and how to trade through a properly authorized broker. Never risk money you cannot afford to lose. Consider qualified professional advice when appropriate.
