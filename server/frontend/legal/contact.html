Contact and Security Reports

Effective August 30, 2026 · Version 1.0
Contact Oryntra AI for account, privacy, security, billing, accessibility, provider-review, or technical questions at silasproff@gmail.com.

What to include

  • Your Oryntra account email, when relevant.
  • The app version, device, browser, and approximate time of the issue.
  • The ticker and feature involved, without sending third-party API keys.
  • Screenshots with personal or credential information removed.

Security reports

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately. Do not publicly disclose credentials, session tokens, private user data, or instructions for bypassing access controls before Oryntra has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate.

Market-data or platform review

Data providers, app reviewers, and business partners may request architecture, public-response examples, entitlement controls, and legal documentation. Oryntra can provide technical evidence of the raw-data boundary, but only the relevant provider or qualified counsel can confirm that a particular license and deployment are authorized.

Do not send secrets

Never email passwords, API keys, payment-card details, authentication codes, database files, or session tokens. Oryntra support does not need them.
