Payments and Refunds

Effective date: July 29, 2026

This policy explains how payments, subscriptions, cancellations, and refund requests are handled when paid Oryntra AI features are offered.
Current Service

Current Payment Status

As of the effective date, Oryntra AI does not directly process payment-card information or charge users through its website.

Free access may be supported by advertising. Viewing or interacting with an advertisement does not create a payment obligation to Oryntra AI.

App Store Purchases

Purchases Processed by Apple

If Oryntra AI offers an in-app purchase or subscription through the Apple App Store, Apple processes the payment and maintains the associated billing information.

App Store purchases are also subject to Apple’s applicable terms, billing rules, cancellation procedures, and refund decisions.

Refund Requests

Requesting an Apple Refund

Refund requests for purchases processed by Apple must generally be submitted directly to Apple. Oryntra AI cannot approve, deny, or directly issue a refund for a transaction controlled by Apple.

To request an eligible refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and follow Apple’s instructions.

Refund eligibility and processing times may vary by country, region, payment method, purchase type, and applicable consumer law.
Subscriptions

Cancellation and Renewal

If a recurring subscription is offered, its price, billing period, renewal terms, and available trial terms will be shown before purchase.

Canceling a subscription generally stops future renewals but does not automatically refund a completed billing period. Access may continue until the end of the paid period, subject to the applicable platform rules.

App Store subscriptions can be managed through the subscription settings associated with the user’s Apple Account.

Billing Problems

Duplicate or Unrecognized Charges

For an App Store charge, first review the Apple receipt and Apple purchase history. Report unrecognized Apple transactions through Apple’s official support and purchase-reporting channels.

You may also contact Oryntra AI support with non-sensitive information about the issue. Do not send complete card numbers, passwords, authentication codes, or banking credentials.

Future Changes

Future Paid Features

If Oryntra AI introduces direct website billing or another payment method, this policy will be updated before that method is used to collect payment.

Any paid offer should identify its price, billing interval, automatic-renewal terms, cancellation method, and material feature limitations before purchase.
