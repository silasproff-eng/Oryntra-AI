Billing, Cancellation, and Refund Policy

Effective August 3, 2026 · Version 0.9
Current status: Oryntra's public paid subscription is not active in this source release. A displayed proposed price or plan is informational until checkout is enabled through an identified payment provider.

Future subscription terms

If Oryntra launches a recurring plan, the purchase screen will state the price, billing interval, included daily request allowance, renewal terms, trial terms, taxes, and the payment provider before purchase. The planned introductory concept is approximately $3 per month with up to 100 ticker-analysis requests per UTC day, but pricing and limits may change before launch.

Cancellation

Subscriptions purchased through Apple's App Store or another platform must be managed through that platform. Cancellation generally prevents future renewal and does not automatically refund a completed billing period. Access may continue until the end of the paid period.

Refunds

Refund eligibility is determined by the payment platform's rules and applicable law. App Store purchase refund requests are handled by Apple. For direct billing introduced later, the checkout terms will identify the refund process and contact channel.

Service and quota changes

Market-data licenses, provider limits, abuse controls, and outages may require temporary service restrictions. Material permanent changes to paid entitlements should be communicated before renewal where reasonably possible.

No manual activation as payment

Developer or beta database flags are not purchases and create no paid entitlement. Never send payment credentials by email or enter them into an Oryntra support form.
