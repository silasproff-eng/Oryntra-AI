Technical Issues

Information to Include

When reporting a technical problem, include any relevant details:

  • The device, browser, or operating system you were using.
  • The Oryntra AI app version, when applicable.
  • The screen or feature where the issue occurred.
  • The ticker symbol involved, if relevant.
  • A description of what you expected and what occurred.
  • A screenshot that does not expose sensitive information.
Account and Privacy

Account or Data Requests

Account-deletion requests should normally be initiated through the deletion controls available on the Oryntra AI website or iOS app. Contact support if those controls are unavailable or if you need help completing a privacy request.

We may need to verify that you control the associated account before completing a request involving account information.

Protect Your Information

Do Not Send Sensitive Credentials

Never email passwords, authentication codes, brokerage credentials, bank-account information, complete payment-card numbers, Social Security numbers, or private API keys.
