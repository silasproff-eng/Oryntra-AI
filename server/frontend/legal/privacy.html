Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026 · Version 1.0
Summary: Oryntra stores the information needed to operate accounts, quotas, watchlists, simulations, notifications, security, and support. It does not require a brokerage connection and does not store brokerage passwords. In the browser-direct provider flow, your Polygon / Massive or Twelve Data key stays in browser memory and is sent directly to that provider. Oryntra's server does not receive, store, log, or return the key. A key does not give Oryntra or you any additional provider-data rights.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: email, display name, salted password hash, legal-consent version, login timestamps, and sessions.
  • Usage: requested ticker, selected lookback, daily request count, feature interactions, errors, device/app metadata, and security logs.
  • User content: watchlists, alert selections, simulated trade-journal entries, notes, and support messages.
  • Browser-direct provider connection: Oryntra does not collect the provider API key. The browser sends the key directly to the provider. Oryntra receives the normalized market bars needed for the analysis you request.
  • Advertising and consent: consent choices and identifiers handled by the applicable advertising platform where enabled.
  • Billing: subscription status and provider references after a payment system is implemented. Oryntra should not store full card numbers.

2. How information is used

We use information to authenticate users, operate features, enforce quotas and licenses, generate requested analysis, synchronize user content, prevent abuse, debug failures, communicate service information, process future subscriptions, and comply with legal or provider obligations. The browser-direct key is not available to Oryntra for any of these purposes.

3. Market-analysis requests and browser-direct provider keys

When you request a ticker analysis through the browser-direct flow, your browser calls the provider with your key and sends the returned market bars to Oryntra for the requested calculation. Oryntra's server never receives, stores, logs, or returns the provider key. The uploaded bars are validated and processed in memory for the request; the browser-direct analysis path does not persist raw OHLCV bars. Oryntra returns derived indicators, patterns, scores, and planning levels rather than downloadable raw history. TradingView separately receives the symbol needed to render its hosted chart and handles that interaction under its own privacy policy.

Your key and requested symbol are transmitted directly from your browser to the provider. Each provider processes that request under its own terms and privacy practices. Oryntra does not represent that a personal, free, or individual provider plan permits commercial use, public display, or redistribution; you must verify that your exact plan and intended use are allowed.

4. Disclosure and service providers

Information may be shared with hosting, database, email, analytics, security, notification, advertising, payment, AI, market-data, and chart providers only as needed for their functions. We may disclose information to comply with law, protect users or the service, investigate abuse, or complete a business transfer. Oryntra does not sell passwords or private account content.

5. Retention and deletion

Account and user-created content is retained while the account is active and for a limited period afterward where needed for backups, security, dispute handling, or legal obligations. Daily usage records may be retained for quota enforcement and service planning. Browser-direct keys disappear when you clear them, sign out, close the session, or reload the page because Oryntra does not store them. Deleting an Oryntra account deletes locally controlled account records subject to those limitations; it does not delete records held independently by TradingView, payment providers, advertisers, or market-data providers.

6. Security

Oryntra uses password hashing, authenticated sessions, access controls, restricted public serialization, and secure transport in production. Provider keys used in the browser-direct flow are kept only in browser memory and are sent directly to the selected provider, not to Oryntra. No system is completely secure. Do not email passwords, API keys, or authentication tokens.

7. Your choices

  • Update or delete watchlists, alerts, and simulations.
  • Clear a browser-direct provider key in Settings, sign out, or reload the page to remove it from Oryntra's browser session.
  • Disable notifications and manage advertising consent where available.
  • Cancel future subscriptions through the provider that processed the purchase.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion through the published contact channel, subject to identity verification and legal limitations.

8. Children

Oryntra is not directed to children under the minimum age required by applicable law or app-store rules. A parent or guardian must supervise any permitted minor use. Oryntra does not knowingly collect data from a child where parental authorization is legally required and absent.
