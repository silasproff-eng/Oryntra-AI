Section 1

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Oryntra AI website, iOS application, account services, market-scanning tools, watchlists, simulated paper-trading features, widgets, notifications, support communications, and related services that link to this policy.

Third-party websites, advertisements, market-data services, and other external services linked through Oryntra AI are governed by their own privacy policies and practices.

Oryntra AI provides market-analysis, educational, watchlist, scanning, and simulated paper-trading tools. Oryntra AI is not a brokerage, bank, registered investment adviser, or payment processor.

Section 4

iOS Application Data

If you enable notifications, Oryntra AI may receive and store an Apple Push Notification service device token. This token is used to deliver notifications to your device. You may disable notifications through your iPhone settings.

The Oryntra AI application and its widget may use an Apple App Group to share limited settings or content required for the widget to function. This information is stored within the application’s designated container on the device.

Embedded web content

Certain application screens may display Oryntra AI or third-party content through an embedded browser or WebView. Embedded content may use cookies, scripts, local storage, cached files, and network requests in a manner similar to a website.

Apple services

Apple may independently process information when you download, install, update, or use the application through Apple services. Apple’s processing is governed by Apple’s own privacy policies.

Section 8

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain accounts, resolve disputes, protect security, prevent fraud, satisfy legal obligations, and enforce agreements.

Users may initiate permanent account deletion through the Oryntra AI website or iOS application. Account deletion is intended to remove the user account and associated active account data, including:

  • Active login sessions.
  • Personal watchlist entries.

Deleting the Oryntra AI application from a device does not automatically delete the associated Oryntra AI account.

Supplemental Disclosure

Alpaca Connect and Market-Data Processing

When a signed-in user chooses to connect an Alpaca account, the user is redirected to Alpaca to authorize access. Oryntra AI does not request or collect the user’s Alpaca password.

The authorization token returned by Alpaca is stored encrypted on the Oryntra server and is used to request market data for that user’s analysis. The mobile application receives Oryntra’s derived analysis and connection status, not the Alpaca access token or raw historical bar arrays.

Users may disconnect an Alpaca environment from the Account tab. They may also need to revoke the application separately through Alpaca. Availability remains subject to Alpaca approval, account eligibility, market-data rights, and provider terms.
