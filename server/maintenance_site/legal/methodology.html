Oryntra AI Transparency

Methodology

Effective date: July 29, 2026

This page provides a general overview of how Oryntra AI produces technical-analysis outputs, simulations, and educational planning information.
Educational analysis only. Oryntra AI outputs are software-generated estimates, not personalized financial advice, trade instructions, probabilities of profit, or guarantees of future performance.
Contents 1. Overview 2. Data Inputs 3. Technical Analysis 4. Scores and Labels 5. AI-Generated Content 6. Paper Trading 7. Backtesting 8. Limitations 9. Independent Verification
Section 1

Overview

Oryntra AI combines technical market data, defined software rules, calculated indicators, pattern-detection methods, and generated summaries to provide educational market-analysis tools.

Methodologies, calculations, data sources, interfaces, and feature availability may change as the service is maintained and improved.

Section 2

Data Inputs

Depending on the feature, the service may evaluate:

  • Historical and recent market prices.
  • Opening, high, low, and closing values.
  • Trading volume and changes in volume.
  • Trend direction and price structure.
  • Momentum and volatility measurements.
  • Support and resistance areas.
  • Selected chart and candlestick patterns.
  • Risk-and-reward planning values.

Data may originate from third-party providers and may be delayed, revised, incomplete, unavailable, or inaccurate.

Section 3

Technical Analysis

Oryntra AI may apply rules, calculations, and statistical methods to identify technical conditions. These conditions may be summarized as trends, setups, patterns, ranges, or planning levels.

Technical analysis is inherently uncertain. A recognized pattern does not establish that a security will move in a particular direction or reach a displayed level.

Section 4

Scores, Confidence Labels, and Rankings

Scores and labels are comparative software outputs based on the factors selected for a feature. They are intended to summarize technical conditions consistently.

A higher score or stronger label does not mean that a trade will be profitable and must not be interpreted as a verified probability of profit unless the service expressly identifies it as such and explains the relevant validation.

Section 5

AI-Generated Content

Some summaries may be generated or organized using artificial intelligence. AI-generated content may misunderstand data, omit relevant context, use incorrect assumptions, or produce inaccurate statements.

AI-generated text should be treated as an educational explanation of available software outputs—not as an independent verification of the underlying information.

Section 6

Simulated Paper Trading

Paper trading is simulated and does not involve real money, securities, brokerage accounts, or trade execution.

Simulated results may not reflect slippage, liquidity, spreads, partial fills, rejected orders, commissions, fees, taxes, margin requirements, market halts, or broker-specific behavior.

Section 7

Backtests and Historical Statistics

Backtests and historical pattern statistics are hypothetical. They may be affected by data quality, limited sample sizes, selection bias, survivorship bias, overfitting, hindsight, changing market conditions, and assumptions about entries, exits, timing, fees, and execution.

Backtested or historical performance does not predict or guarantee future performance.

Section 8

Important Limitations

  • Displayed prices may not be current executable prices.
  • Signals and patterns may be incorrectly identified.
  • Calculated levels may not be reached.
  • Market conditions can change without warning.
  • Historical relationships may stop behaving as expected.
  • Software, network, provider, and calculation errors can occur.
Section 9

Independent Verification

Users should independently verify prices, company information, market conditions, calculations, and risks using appropriate professional and primary sources.

Oryntra AI does not connect to brokerage accounts or execute real-money transactions. Users remain solely responsible for their own financial decisions.
